Advanced
Map construction principle. Kriging fits an exponential variogram to each frame's samples, weights noisy measurements via the nugget effect and relaxes to the field mean away from data. LPI fits a Gaussian-weighted local plane at every grid node (σ = 200 km) — with ordinary kriging, one of the two most accurate local methods in comparative studies. Degree 2 fits a local quadric where the station neighbourhood is dense enough (≥7 within ~2σ) and falls back to the plane elsewhere; it can track the midday TEC bump better but is more sensitive to station biases.
VTEC, its spatial-gradient magnitude, or propagation fields derived pointwise from VTEC (GDD, B_k).
Carrier frequency used only when Field is GDD or B_k.
Rolling-median window per (station, satellite, arc). Reduces single-epoch noise; does not fix inter-station bias.
Per-station median-shift over samples with elevation ≥ 60° — kills the "wall" artefact when a receiver's DCB is uncorrected.
Climatological reference from the IRI model (PyIRI). The daily F10.7 index is fetched automatically (spaceweather.gc.ca, cached on the server). IRI reproduces the background and diurnal course, not ionospheric weather; the empirical relative VTEC keeps residual inter-station biases, so a systematic offset in the difference is expected — it is reported as bias next to RMSE. The series export adds IRI columns when this is not off.
